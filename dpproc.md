# Navaratri Ghatasthapana: Simple Home Puja
## What you need

- [ ] Clay or brass pot (kalash) and a wide plate or tray
- [ ] Clean soil, and barley or mixed grains
- [ ] Ganga jal, red thread (mouli)
- [ ] 5 mango or ashoka leaves
- [ ] Unbroken rice (akshat)
- [ ] 5 coins
- [ ] Coconut and red cloth
- [ ] Flowers, sandalwood paste, incense, ghee lamp
- [ ] Photo of Ma Durga (optionally Sri Ramakrishna and Holy Mother)

## Day 1: Set up the kalash

1. Spread a layer of soil on the plate. Sprinkle grains. Repeat for 3 layers. Sprinkle a little water.
2. Tie the red thread 9 times around the kalash neck.
3. Draw a swastika on the kalash with red powder.
4. Put the 5 coins inside. Fill with water up to the neck.
5. Add a few drops of Ganga jal.
6. Place the 5 leaves around the rim. Keep stems out of the water.
7. Put a lid or small plate on top. Fill it with rice.
8. Wrap the coconut in red cloth. Tie it with thread. Place it on the rice, tuft facing you.
9. Set it up in a clean spot. Do not move it for 9 days.

## Day 1: Invoke Ma

Do this before sunset on Pratipada, ideally in the Ghatasthapana muhurat (check Drik Panchang).

1. Light the lamp and incense.
2. Sip water (achamana). Sprinkle a little around you and say:
   *Om apavitraḥ pavitro vā sarvāvasthāṃ gato'pi vā, yaḥ smaret puṇḍarīkākṣaṃ sa bāhyābhyantaraḥ śuciḥ.*
3. Pray to Ganesha:
   *Vakratuṇḍa mahākāya sūryakoṭi samaprabha, nirvighnaṃ kuru me deva sarvakāryeṣu sarvadā.*
4. Offer a flower or a pinch of rice to Ganesha, your Guru, and your Ishta (chosen deity).
5. Take your sankalpa (vow): "I will worship Ma Durga at this kalash for 9 days." Add any japa or stotra you plan to do.
6. Invite Ma into the kalash. Offer a flower and say:
   *Om hrīṁ durgāyai namaḥ, āvāhayāmi.*
7. Imagine the kalash as Ma herself. Treat it as her living form from now on.

## Daily puja (Days 1 to 9)

1. Light the lamp. Sip water.
2. Pray to Ganesha and your Guru.
3. Dhyana (meditation): picture Ma on her lion, radiant and merciful.
   *Sarvamaṅgala-maṅgalye śive sarvārtha-sādhike, śaraṇye tryambake gauri nārāyaṇi namo'stu te.*
4. Offer five things, each with *Om hrīṁ durgāyai namaḥ ___ samarpayāmi*:
   - Sandalwood paste (gandham)
   - Flower (puṣpam)
   - Incense (dhūpam)
   - Lamp (dīpam)
   - Food (naivedyam)
5. Chant a Durga stotra, or read from the Chandi.
6. Do your daily count of whichever Durga mantra/stotram you have taken sankalpa of. 
7. After completing the desired japa count, meditate on the following shloka for 5-10 mins 
 minimum. Can meditate longer as per capacity. 
** ॐ शरणागत दीनार्त परित्राण परायणे । सर्वस्यार्तिहरे देवि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
Salutations to Devi Narayani, who is utterly dedicated to rescuing the helpless and distressed who seek refuge in Her, and who removes the suffering of all beings.

8. Sit in silence for 5 to 10 minutes.
9. Do arati.
10. Offer the day's work to Ma.
11. Water the grains lightly each day. Keep them from getting soggy.

> Short on time? Do steps 1, 3, 4, 6, and 9.

## Day 8 or 9: Kumari Puja (optional)

- Feed and honour young girls as forms of Ma.
- Give them a small gift.
- If you can't, give a saree or sweets to a Durga temple.

## Day 10: Visarjan

1. Do the daily puja one last time.
2. Thank Ma. Offer new cloth or sweets.
3. Ask forgiveness:
   *Āvāhanaṃ na jānāmi na jānāmi visarjanam, pūjāṃ caiva na jānāmi kṣamyatāṃ parameśvari.*
   *Mantra-hīnaṃ kriyā-hīnaṃ bhakti-hīnaṃ sureśvari, yat pūjitaṃ mayā devi paripūrṇaṃ tad astu me.*
4. Remove the coconut. Place it with the red cloth in running water.
5. Sprinkle the kalash water around your home with the leaves.
6. Put the grain sprouts in running water, or under a clean tree.
7. Leave the rice out for birds.
8. Tie the coins and a little rice in red cloth. Keep it in your puja space.
